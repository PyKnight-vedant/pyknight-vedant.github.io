About

Two threads: how markets behave, and how machines learn.

One thread is econophysics — applying statistical mechanics to financial markets. Non-Gaussian behavior in returns is well established; what's less understood is how multifractal structure varies across sectors, asset classes, and regimes. My published research uses MF-DFA, spectral cumulant analysis, and box-counting fractal dimensions across 50 assets and 6 sectors to characterize that heterogeneity, and connects it to diffusion and chaotic dynamics.

The other is applied AI/ML — agentic pipelines with LangGraph, deep learning across NLP, computer vision, and audio, and generative models (GANs, diffusion). I've built these in production internships and research settings, from schema-inference agents on Databricks to deepfake detection and brain tumor segmentation.

The two increasingly intersect — I'm exploring how multifractal market statistics could inform the generative models used to simulate markets — and I'm actively pursuing roles and graduate programs across both quantitative research and AI/ML.
